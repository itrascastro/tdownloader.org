Network transparency

TDownloader runs entirely on your computer. Here is the exact list of everything the app connects to, why, and what is sent, so you can verify it yourself.

Verify it yourself

You can watch every connection the app makes, live, with a network monitor. You will only see the ones listed on this page.

  • Wireshark (any system)
  • Little Snitch or LuLu (macOS)
  • OpenSnitch (Linux)
  • GlassWire or Windows Firewall (Windows)

Not sure how? Follow our step-by-step guide to audit any app.

Telegram, essential

It is the core of the app: signing in, listing your channels, downloading files, and uploading or organizing in TCloud. Your session lives on your disk and talks directly to Telegram's servers. We are not a middleman: your account and your files never pass through us.

TDownloader.org, updates and downloads

The app checks whether a new version is available, receives its basic configuration, and downloads updates. These are read-only requests: we send no information about you or how you use the app.

If you block this domain in your firewall, the app keeps working normally; you only stop receiving automatic updates. That is the proof that the core does not depend on our servers.

Anonymous statistics, only if you enable them

They are off by default. Nothing at all is sent unless you tick the consent checkbox.

If you enable them, we send a random, anonymous identifier (which identifies neither you nor your account), the app version, the operating system, and the language. Never your Telegram account, your files, your channels, or any personal data.

You can revoke them at any time in Settings.

Problem reports, only if you send them

When you use "Report a problem" we only receive the text you write (and a contact, if you choose to include one). Never automatically.

What TDownloader never does

  • Send your Telegram session or credentials anywhere.
  • Upload your files to our servers: in TCloud, your content goes to your own Telegram channel.
  • Include third-party trackers or ad networks.
  • Sell or share data, or profile you.

In short

Everything happens on your computer. The app talks to Telegram ( just like the official client ) and to our domain only to update itself. Statistics are anonymous and optional, and you can verify it yourself with a network monitor.
