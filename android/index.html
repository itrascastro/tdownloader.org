TDownloader for Telegram - Early access for Android

Catalog, search and download files from your Telegram channels. Turn busy channels into a searchable library on your phone.

Free early access. Two quick steps. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

What is TDownloader?

Many Telegram channels and groups are full of files: videos, documents, audio and books. Finding and saving them means scrolling through endless messages.

TDownloader turns the channels you already follow into a searchable catalog on your phone. Search and filter by name, type, size or date, pick what you want and download it. It all works with your own Telegram account.

A look inside

What you can do

Add Telegram channels and groups you already have access to.

Index their files into a searchable local catalog on your phone.

Filter by name, title, type, extension, size and date.

Select one file or many results and create download projects.

Pause, resume, retry and cancel downloads.

Keep files in a private in-app library and export them when you need.
