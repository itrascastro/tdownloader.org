Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. What data we collect

TDownloader for Android runs entirely on your device. We do not collect any data. We have no servers of our own, and the app has no analytics, telemetry or advertising.

The only connection the app makes is to Telegram, using your own account, to sign in and download the content you ask for. That traffic goes directly between your device and Telegram.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything the app needs stays on your device:

  • Telegram session (authentication token encrypted by Telegram)
  • App settings (destination folder, language, added channels)
  • Downloaded files and the local catalog index

Everything stays on your device and there is no cloud backup. You can delete it all at any time from Android Settings, under Apps > TDownloader > Storage, or by uninstalling the app, which removes the data.

3. No tracking

The app contains no cookies, no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no usage analytics.

4. Third-party services

Content is downloaded directly from Telegram's servers using your own account. Your use of Telegram is subject to Telegram's own privacy policy. TDownloader is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Telegram.

5. Permissions

The app uses internet access to connect to Telegram and notification permission to let you know when a download finishes. It does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

6. Minors

TDownloader is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing it periodically.

8. Contact

For any privacy question you can contact us through our website, tdownloader.org.
