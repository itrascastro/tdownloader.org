Built for everyday organization

TCloud is not a separate storage service. It is an organized layer over your own Telegram account.

Virtual folders

Create folders inside each TCloud space and move files, notes and links without losing the Telegram source.

Tags everywhere

Add semantic tags to files, folders, notes and links, then combine them with search and filters.

Visible notes

Notes appear in Telegram as readable messages and can be created or edited from Telegram or TDownloader.

Uploads with progress

Upload individual files or whole folders with a queue, progress, speed, destination and cancellation.

Channels as spaces

Use the managed TDownloader space, link your own channels or import Saved Messages.

ZIP downloads

Download selected files and folders as a ZIP while preserving the virtual structure.

How it works

1

Your files stay between your computer and Telegram

TDownloader does not proxy your content through our servers. The app uses your own Telegram account and stores the TCloud state in Telegram so another computer can restore it automatically.
